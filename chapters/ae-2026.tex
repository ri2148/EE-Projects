	\item 
How many 3-digit numbers can be formed using three distinct single digit prime numbers?
\hfill (GATE AE 2026)
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $64$
    \item $24$
    \item $12$
    \item $4$
\end{enumerate} 
\solution
 There are 4 single digit prime numbers: 2,3,5 and 7. The desired numbers are given by
 \begin{align}
	 \perm{4}{3} = \frac{4!}{1!} = 24
 \end{align}
\end{enumerate}
